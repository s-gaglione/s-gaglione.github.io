About

I’m a CTO with a background in product design and data. My research aims at concrete uses: company knowledge, compliance, public services, education. Cases where an answer must be checkable, correctable, and able to run on premises.

How I work: I separate the measures chosen before an experiment from those I add while analysing the first results, and I publish what fails too. I share what I learn from my experiments; the architecture of my systems stays confidential.

Written with the help of AI tools. The experiments and the opinions are mine.
